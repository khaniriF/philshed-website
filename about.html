Our story

A strong foundation, built in Voi since 2014

Philshed Learning Center opened in 2014 on the old Mombasa Road in Voi town, founded by Phidilia Mwahe. Today, the school teaches children from PP1 to Grade 6 under the CBE curriculum.

Learners also study French and ICT, and the school makes room for music and the arts. In 2026, Philshed reached the Nationals at the music festival.

Our motto reflects the belief that what a child learns in the early years helps shape everything that follows.
