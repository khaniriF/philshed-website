Our story

A strong foundation, built in Voi since 2014

Philshed Learning Center opened in 2014 on the old Mombasa road in Voi town, founded by Phidilia Mwahe. Today we teach children from PP1 to Grade 6 under the CBE curriculum.

Our learners also study French and ICT, and in 2026 the school reached the Nationals at the music festival. The school day runs from 7:20am to 4:00pm.

Philshed began in 2014 with a simple belief from our founder, Phidilia Mwahe: that what a child learns in the early years shapes everything that follows. That belief is in our motto, Strong Foundation for a Brighter Future, and it is how we teach every child, from their first day in PP1 to their last in Grade 6.
